Command your formation by voice. Give the preparatory command ("Column right…"), then emphasize the command of execution ("MARCH!"). Make it louder, change your pitch on it, or pause briefly before it.
Recruit: any one of those counts. Soldier: any two. Drill Sergeant: all of them, plus a short, sharp execution called on the correct foot. Commands only: no voice check, for warm-up.
Quick list: Forward, MARCH · Platoon, HALT · Column right/left, MARCH · Column half right/left, MARCH · Right/Left flank, MARCH · Rear, MARCH · Counter column, MARCH · Mark time, MARCH · Half step, MARCH · Quick/Double time, MARCH · Change step, MARCH · Right/Left step, MARCH · Backward, MARCH · Right/Left/About, FACE · Parade, REST · Stand at, EASE · AT EASE · Platoon, ATTENTION · Dress right, DRESS · Eyes, RIGHT · Ready, FRONT · Open/Close ranks, MARCH · Extend/Close, MARCH · Fall, IN.

Snake: pick up recruits (gold) to grow the formation. Marching into a wall or into your own formation = FORMATION BROKE.

Command Reference

Based on TC 3-21.5, Drill and Ceremonies. Foot is the foot that should be striking the ground when you call the command of execution. Squad, Platoon, and Flight are all accepted as the unit word.
